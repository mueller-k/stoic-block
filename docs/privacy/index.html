Stoic Block privacy policy

Stoic Block replaces supported top-level visits to sites you choose with a bundled quote. This policy describes the extension; the linked websites have their own practices.

Information stored on your device

Your chosen hostnames, blocking preference, consent, and configuration revisions are stored in local extension storage in your Chrome profile. A previous configuration may be stored temporarily for recovery. Chrome stores dynamic redirect rules derived from your blocklist. The extension does not sync this configuration or apply its own encryption.

Site access and browsing activity

HTTP and HTTPS site access allows Chrome to redirect supported top-level navigations to the hostnames you choose and their subdomains. Stoic Block does not record visited URLs, browsing history, visit counts, or quote selections, and does not send browsing activity to us.

Network use

Quotes, code, and other extension assets are bundled; the interface uses system fonts. The extension does not make external requests as part of its operation. Chrome installation, updates, and other browser services are separate and may use the network. Links to the online policy, support, and quote sources open external websites only when you select them; those sites may process requests under their own policies.

Retention and removal

Settings remain in your Chrome profile until you remove or reset them, or remove the extension. Removing a site removes it from the blocklist; disabling blocking preserves your saved sites. The recovery screen offers a reset of the blocklist and settings. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage and browser-managed rules.

Coverage

Incognito is excluded. Already-open pages remain, and some offline, service-worker, restored history, and protected browser pages may not be redirected. Stoic Block is a focus aid, not a security control.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, visit Stoic Block issues on GitHub. Opening this link requires an internet connection and GitHub may process your request.
