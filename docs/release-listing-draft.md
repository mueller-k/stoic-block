# Chrome Web Store listing draft

Draft for maintainer review. This is not approval to publish or a statement that the listed behavior has passed the release audit. Resolve the policy questions in the release status before submission.

## Listing copy

**Name:** Stoic Block

**Short description:** Replace distracting site visits with a quiet Stoic quote. Your blocklist stays local.

**Detailed description:** Choose the sites that distract you and preview the hostname and subdomains each entry covers. When Chrome redirects a supported navigation to a saved site, Stoic Block displays a bundled historical quote. Remove sites or disable blocking from settings at any time.

Your blocklist and settings stay in your Chrome profile. Stoic Block does not record your browsing activity or send it to us. Quotes are bundled; the extension makes no external requests during operation. Chrome services and links you choose to open are separate.

Incognito is excluded. Already-open pages remain, and some offline, service-worker, history-restored, and protected browser pages may not be redirected. Stoic Block is a focus aid, not a security control.

**Support:** https://github.com/mueller-k/stoic-block/issues

**Proposed privacy URL:** https://mueller-k.github.io/stoic-block/privacy/ (the static source is `docs/privacy/index.html`; GitHub Pages must be configured and the published URL verified before use).

**Distribution:** United States only. Select this intentionally in the publisher dashboard; do not infer worldwide rights from the US quote review.
