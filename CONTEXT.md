# Stoic Block

Stoic Block helps people avoid distracting sites by replacing blocked visits with a minimalist page containing a Stoic quote.

## Language

**Blocklist**:
The single collection of sites a person chooses to block.
_Avoid_: Lists, blocklists, site groups

**Quote page**:
The minimalist page shown in place of a blocked site, containing a Stoic quote related to focus, growth, or learning. It offers no bypass of the block.

**Blocked site**:
A hostname included in the blocklist, covering that hostname and all its subdomains, whose supported visits are redirected to the quote page while blocking is enabled. An entered subdomain does not include its parent domain.
