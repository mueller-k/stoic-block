STOIC BLOCK

Your settings stay here.

This is the privacy explanation for the development version. Final public-release policy and Chrome Web Store disclosures remain subject to verification and maintainer approval.

What is stored

Stoic Block saves your chosen hostname, whether blocking is enabled, your consent, and the configuration version in your Chrome profile’s local extension storage. Chrome also retains redirect rules derived from that configuration. Configuration is not synced by the extension.

Why broad site access is requested

Access to HTTP and HTTPS sites lets Chrome redirect supported top-level visits to sites you choose and their subdomains. The extension does not use visit callbacks, record visited URLs, keep activity counts, or send browsing activity to us.

Local operation

Quotes and all extension assets are bundled. There are no accounts, analytics, remote fonts, remote quotes, or extension-originated external requests. Chrome’s installation, updates, and other browser services are separate and may use the network.

Removal and limitations

Disabling the extension in Chrome stops its redirects. Removing it removes its local extension storage. This development version does not yet offer blocklist editing or global disable controls inside the extension. Incognito is excluded, and some offline or restored pages may not be redirected.

Local storage is not claimed to have application-level encryption. Store-policy interpretation and final release privacy evidence are pending.
